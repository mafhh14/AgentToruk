# Using the chat

## Open the chat

Click the chat bubble in the bottom corner of the website.

## First visit

You may be asked for your name and email. This helps support follow up if needed.

## Send a message

Type your question in plain language, for example:

- "What's your return policy?"
- "Where is my order #12345?"
- "I need to talk to someone about billing"

Press **Enter** or click **Send**.

## Upload files

Use the attachment button to share screenshots, receipts, or documents (when enabled).

## Talk to a human

Click **Talk to a human** if you prefer speaking with a support agent. Your conversation history is passed along.

## Rate your experience

When you close the chat, you may be asked to rate the conversation.
