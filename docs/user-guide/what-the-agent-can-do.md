# What the agent can do

The AgentToruk chat agent helps website visitors by:

- **Answering questions** from your company knowledge base (policies, FAQs, guides)
- **Checking status** on orders, subscriptions, or accounts (when integrated)
- **Creating support tickets** when issues need human follow-up
- **Collecting information** for refunds, appointments, or bug reports
- **Escalating to a human** when the AI cannot resolve the issue

## What to expect

- Responses include **source references** when answering from documents
- The agent **asks follow-up questions** when it needs more details
- You can click **"Talk to a human"** at any time
- You can **rate the conversation** when closing the chat

## Limitations

The agent only knows what your business has configured — uploaded documents, integrations, and rules. It will not make up policies or promise actions it cannot perform.
